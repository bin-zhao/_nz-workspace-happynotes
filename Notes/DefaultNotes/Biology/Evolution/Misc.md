## Penetrant

A **penetrant** is a substance or agent that is able to penetrate or seep into something else. The term can refer to different things depending on the field: 

- **In chemistry and mechanics:** A penetrant is a liquid formula designed to break through rust and corrosion on stuck parts, like bolts and screws. It has a low viscosity and uses capillary action to enter small cracks and joints.
- **In genetics:** The term is used in the context of a gene's **penetrance**, which describes the likelihood that a person who has a disease-causing genetic mutation will actually develop the disease. A "fully penetrant" mutation is one where everyone with the mutation will show the signs and symptoms of the disease.
- **In non-destructive testing (NDT):** A liquid penetrant is a dye used to inspect materials for surface-breaking defects like cracks and pores.
- **In biology and agriculture:** It can refer to a chemical that increases the ability of a substance, such as a herbicide, to penetrate a surface like a plant or organism.
